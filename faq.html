CentaurOne Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CentaurOne grain monitoring system?

CentaurOne is a technologically advanced grain monitoring system that uses wireless sensor technology, Digital Twin modeling, and AI intelligence to monitor and predict grain conditions throughout your storage bin.

Unlike conventional grain monitoring systems that rely on multiple temperature sensors suspended on cables inside the grain, CentaurOne uses a single externally mounted Arrow sensor for bins up to 50,000 bushels.

Can CentaurOne be installed when my grain bin is full?

Yes. CentaurOne's wireless Arrow sensor mounts on the outside wall of your grain bin, with a tubular probe extending into the stored grain. Unlike traditional grain temperature cable systems that typically require installation before the bin is filled, the Arrow sensor can be installed even when your bin is full.

There's no need to wait until the next unloading cycle.

How does CentaurOne monitor grain temperature and moisture without traditional sensor cables?

CentaurOne uses an externally mounted sensor, called the Arrow, to collect real-time grain condition measurements, including moisture, temperature, and CO₂.

Digital Twin technology combines these real-time sensor readings, weather conditions, and grain characteristics to calculate estimated temperature and moisture conditions throughout the grain mass.

This provides whole-bin predictive visibility without requiring traditional temperature monitoring cables.

How is CentaurOne different from conventional grain temperature cable systems?

Traditional grain monitoring systems use multiple cables with physical sensors positioned at various locations inside the grain. These cables typically must be installed when the grain bin is empty.

CentaurOne uses real-time wireless Arrow sensor data and Digital Twin technology to model conditions throughout the bin, including areas without physical sensors.

This approach reduces the amount of hardware installed inside the bin while providing farmers with predictive grain management information.

Can CentaurOne automatically control my grain bin aeration fans?

Yes. CentaurOne supports remote and automated aeration fan management.

Its technology evaluates grain conditions and outside weather to help determine when fan operation can improve stored grain conditions.

Farmers can also monitor and control connected fans remotely through the CentaurOne dashboard.

What size grain bins can CentaurOne monitor?

CentaurOne is primarily designed for farm grain bins up to 50,000 bushels, typically requiring just one Arrow sensor per bin.

Larger bins and commercial grain storage applications may require additional sensors or customized system configurations.

Contact CentaurOne for a custom quote for larger installations.

How long does CentaurOne installation take?

CentaurOne is designed for straightforward installation, typically requiring approximately 15–30 minutes per bin for the sensor installation.

The Arrow sensor mounts externally on the grain bin wall, eliminating the need to install traditional temperature cables inside the grain.

Gateway installation, electrical connections, and fan control setup may require additional time.
