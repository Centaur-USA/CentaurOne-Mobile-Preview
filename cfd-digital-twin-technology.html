CFD Digital Twin Technology for Grain Monitoring

CentaurOne uses advanced Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) Digital Twin technology to create a virtual model of the grain stored inside your bin. By combining actual measurements from the Arrow sensor with weather conditions, grain characteristics, and airflow patterns, the system calculates estimated grain conditions at thousands of locations throughout the bin. This provides a more complete picture of stored grain conditions without requiring dozens of physical sensors.
