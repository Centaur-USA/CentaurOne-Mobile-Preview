3. Priority Actions

  1. Aeration: Consider cooling during suitable outside-air conditions; check local temperature, humidity, and the bin's airflow characteristics before running fans.
  2. Moisture: Verify grain moisture at multiple accessible locations using safe sampling methods.
  3. Inspection: Check for condensation, crusting or unusual odors from safe external access points. Never enter a grain bin without proper procedures and equipment.
  4. Data: Confirm whether relative humidity and other telemetry streams are available before relying on them for decisions.

4. Technical Insights

Warm grain combined with borderline moisture can increase the risk of moisture migration, condensation, insects and localized spoilage. CO₂ is a useful warning indicator, but no single reading confirms grain quality throughout the entire bin.

Practical principle: Grain moisture × temperature × storage time all affect risk. Fan operating decisions should account for weather, airflow and the intended storage period.
